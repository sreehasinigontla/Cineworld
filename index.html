Storyline & Synopsis

In the late 21st century, global crop blights and relentless dust storms threaten human survival. Former NASA pilot Joseph Cooper is recruited for a top-secret mission aboard the endurance spacecraft Lazarus. Crossing through a gravitational anomaly near Saturn, Cooper and his scientific team must navigate relativistic time dilation, rogue ocean worlds, and moral dilemmas where every hour spent on an alien planet costs seven years back on Earth.
